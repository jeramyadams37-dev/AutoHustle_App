# AutoHustle Franchise Operations Manual (v1.0)

## 1. The "Spark" Drop Protocol
* **Location:** Target Walmart/Target parking lots during peak waiting times (XX:40 - XX:55).
* **Action:** Identify drivers (waiting with phones out, hazard lights, or visible app screens).
* **Script:** "Hey, I'm a Spark driver too. I started a mobile mechanic service so we don't lose shift money. First diagnostic is free."

## 2. Vehicle Intake Protocol
1.  **Scan:** Connect OBDII scanner immediately.
2.  **Photo:** Take photo of VIN and Odometer.
3.  **Log:** Enter data into `maintenance_logic.py`.

## 3. The "Pit Crew" Standard
* **Uniform:** Black t-shirt, Red hat (Logo visible).
* **Speed:** Diagnostics must be completed in under 7 minutes.
* **Cleanliness:** No oil spots left in parking lots. Use the "Drip Mat" at all times.
